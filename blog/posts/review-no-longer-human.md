# No Longer Human - Osamu Dazai

7/10

start date: 13 november 2024

finish date: 25 november 2024

i have little inkling of what this book is really about. i could only attempt to fathom. the persona, yozo, slowly descends into self-deprecation and self-loathe by alienating himself from the "outside world". he first plays the role of an actor in a comedy show, then unassociates himself from society by finding the differences in thought between him and those around him. he seems to be overly analytic and his precocity has drawn much unwarranted troubles to him at an early age. 

he repeatedly mentions he cannot refuse offers from people and also does everything in his power to please those around him. he mentions it is due to a terminal fear of something. was it "society" and the pressure surrounding that? or was it not fitting in ?

my honest feeling is that he started out on a wrong path and having had no one to talk to him about it early on, he went on to stray himself and reinforce his decision to ostracise himself from the crowds. this isolation grew with the vices and fuelled his subsequent sslf loathing.

venus says this straying of the path could be possibly due to the sexual abuse he experienced as a child. i dont remember much about the front parts so i cannot ascertain as well. 

his true self is exposed multiple times throughout his life and each time it has made him grow increasingly anxious: the guy pointing out his faked fall in school; the policeman pointing out his faked bleed-coughing. whatever
