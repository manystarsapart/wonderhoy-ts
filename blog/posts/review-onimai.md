# お兄ちゃんはおしまい! / 别当欧尼酱了！/ Onimai: I'm Now Your Sister!

8.5/10

start date: 8 december 2024

finish date: 11 december 2024

slice of life!

went on a vacation to japan and naturally came the mini anime binge. i thoroughly enjoyed this first season of onimai. unlike the classics of literature where there is deep thought and whatnot involved, this is just a really cute and youthful breather from everything in real life. you could call it a vacation for the mind.

plot: mahiro (真尋) wakes up one day and finds that his little sister has turned him into a her. what a premise. im hooked

all the 12 episodes revolve around how mahiro now deals with being a little girl rather than a stayhome good-for-nothing lazy guy. mihari (みはり, his younger sister who skipped grades and is in university and is an absolute nerd) is now the "older sister" character and mahiro is now the "younger sister". at first he struggles to grasp the feminine lifestyle but after relentless effort by mihari and kaede (枫, miharis now-JK ex-classmate) mahiro grows accustomed to looking and acting cute (as he should be. hes so cute. he? she? she.)

mahiro then meets momiji (椛, kaedes little sister) and subsequently transfers to middle school where he meets asahi (朝日) and miyo (美夜) and become a tightly-knit friend group. they then do cute things together and have fun in general. momiji plays the tomboyish character, kaede the JK ara-ara character, asahi the dog-ish joyous character and miyo the 磕 (yuri) CP character. 

not much to say because the series is just me watching the interactions and smiling all the time. thoroughly enjoyed all the gaiety. please drop season 2 soon.
